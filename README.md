## The problems Aptelly solves

| Common problem | How Aptelly helps |
|---|---|
| **Install sources are hard to find.** Your TV may not include Google Play, and the manufacturer store may not carry the app you want. | Aptelly organizes popular TV apps, TV app stores and trusted entry points in one place, so you do not have to search the web with a remote. |
| **It is easy to download the wrong build.** A search result may be a mobile build, the wrong CPU architecture or an app that cannot be controlled with a D-pad. | Aptelly looks at the TV environment and presents a more suitable store or installation route. If no suitable route is available, it says so clearly. |
| **Installing on a television is awkward.** URLs are tedious to type, web pages are difficult to navigate and downloaded files can be hard to locate. | Each app card gives you a direct action such as `Open`, `Find install source` or `Update`, reducing unnecessary steps. |
| **Apps are difficult to manage after installation.** Icons may be scattered across the original launcher or buried in menus. | Aptelly gathers installed TV apps into one large-screen interface designed for remote control. |

> **Being listed does not guarantee that an app will install or play on every TV.** Availability still depends on the television model, Android version, CPU architecture, Google services, DRM certification, country or region, and the provider’s account or subscription requirements. Aptelly makes the route and status clearer; it does not bypass those restrictions.

## Get started

1. Open [Aptelly Releases](https://github.com/AptellyTV/aptellyTV/releases/latest) and download `Aptelly-<version>-release.apk`.
2. Install it using a USB drive, ADB or a trusted download tool already available on your TV. Android may ask you to allow installation from that source.
3. Open Aptelly and select an app. You will see `Open`, `Find install source`, `Update`, or a clear explanation of why it is currently unavailable on that TV.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Can every catalog app be installed directly?</strong></summary>
<br>
Not necessarily. Android version, CPU, available app stores, Google services, DRM and regional availability vary between televisions. Aptelly presents a suitable route when one is known and clearly reports when the current conditions are not met.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Aptelly provide movies, TV shows or subscription accounts?</strong></summary>
<br>
No. Aptelly only organizes entry points to third-party TV apps. Content, accounts, advertising, subscriptions and membership fees remain the responsibility of each provider.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use Aptelly on a TV without Google Play?</strong></summary>
<br>
You can install Aptelly, but each third-party app still depends on the capabilities of your television. The catalog includes manufacturer stores, Aurora Store, Aptoide TV and official project sources where appropriate.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Aptelly silently install other apps?</strong></summary>
<br>
No. Android displays the final installation confirmation, and you can always cancel it.
</details>
